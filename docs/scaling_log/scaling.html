Every model with a parameter count, on a log parameter axis (the depth cells sit at the same budget as their depth-2 twins: S-d4 at 611k). Four lines connect the base recipe: the old lr 1e‑4 sweep, the deployed 3e‑4 at 96k, the 72k corpus, and in gold our maximum 144k corpus.

Second-order hyperparameters are worth nearly as much as every width change at maximum data.

Gold is the margin over MC‑LCB; red and green are the same checkpoints against the current leader. The offline ordering does not survive. h1024 is the best offline model here and only third against the leader; h2048 is second offline and worst in play. It adds the only interval you can trust, because the two gold margins share one production draw and their errors do not add.
