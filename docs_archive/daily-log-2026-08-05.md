## Strength and deployment

The frozen-current paired confirmation used 504 fresh deal clusters:

| contrast | paired signed level utility |
|---|---:|
| N=30 minus N=10 | `+0.222 +/- 0.140` |
| N=30 minus null | `+0.230 +/- 0.139` |
| null minus N=10 | `-0.008 +/- 0.154` |

This reproduced the earlier version-pinned `+0.262 +/- 0.154` result after the

to 36ms/decision for compiled N=30: more verified search at lower latency.

N=60 versus N=30 was `-0.002 +/- 0.119`. This is a failed superiority test,
not proof of equivalence, but it gives no reason to repeat uniform dose scaling
without a different mechanism.

### Correctness did not independently prove strength

A same-engine comparison between current MC and the pre-fix bot layer measured
`-0.054 +/- 0.156`; it did not establish a strength difference and was
protocol-provisional because the old bot produced zero-world decisions.

It did quantify the correctness gain. The pre-fix layer rejected 2,507 of
153,198 sampled worlds (1.64%) and produced four zero-world decisions; current
MC rejected 2 of 30,757 (0.0065%) and produced none. That is about a 252x
reduction in invalid proposals. Correctness and strength are separate axes:
today moved the former dramatically even where the latter interval included
zero.

## Sampler and certification

### Posterior bias was measured, not hand-waved away

The exact reference was corrected from uniform-over-multisets to
uniform-over-physical-deals. On enumerable states:

- baseline mean excess TV was material (`0.161`, 6/8 initial states);
- weighting suit-count splits reduced excess TV by `0.060 +/- 0.031` but did
  not eliminate it and was too slow for the hot path;
- uniform cap-respecting per-card choice contributed essentially zero
  (`-0.0001 +/- 0.0027`);
- the remaining bias is not yet fully attributed.

The decision-sensitivity screen then separated posterior error from finite-N
noise. Most enumerable endgames were decision-degenerate; among 30 live states,
aggregate excess disagreement/regret included zero, but individual states swung
materially in both directions. Therefore the bias is neither harmless nor a
demonstrated systematic strength loss. All experimental sampler flags remain
OFF.

The durable distinction is:

- for **correctness and training labels**, the true physical-deal posterior is
  still open;
- for the completed **policy screen**, freezing the production sampler as part
  of every compared policy was a legitimate estimand.

### The P0 population claim was reopened

The original `eea78d2` artifact was described as 1,600 original+late states.
Audit showed `reservoir_states()` maintained one global limit: the 20,845-row
original source exhausted it, so zero late rows were tested. It also had no
named skip counters. A first replacement repeated the same population defect
and ran only 40 of the registered 120 toys.

The certifier now counts four replay/drop paths and fails closed on skips.
Commit `fc19d26` also makes original, late and deep mandatory, resets counts per
source, enforces exact 500/500/500 quotas, records them, refuses short/missing
sources and requires 120 toys. The first 1,500-state v2 run produced 36,000
accepted, zero rejected/invalid/skipped worlds and 120/120 toy support, but it
was generated before commit and records `tree_dirty=true`.

P0 is therefore not closed tonight. Before closure, `certified` must explicitly
require zero rejected worlds and `accepted == requested`, the default toy count
must match 120, and the full artifact must rerun from clean current compiled+
strict code. Even that bounded certificate will not prove the production
dealer globally complete or the posterior correctly weighted.

- The 20,845-state high-N corpus is a replayable state reservoir, not an oracle:

  points and heuristic continuation.
- The 12,000-state late supplement fixes coverage—ply>=20 supply increased by
  about 10.9x—but remains raw state data until relabelled under a current,
  explicit contract.
- Evaluation assets are now inventoried separately from training data. The
  frozen DEV/CALIB sets are exams, not supervised corpora.
- `v11pair` remains the best learned result: 57.7% versus SmartBot and useful as
  a direct override/root ranker on its exact ballot. It has no seeded proof over
  MC and is invalid as a cross-state leaf because pairwise deltas lack absolute
  scale.
- DMC2's halted runs do not reject AWAC/DouZero/Suphx ideas. The implementation
  mixes defender-signed returns with an attacker-perspective oracle and the old
  residual recipe is not a faithful implementation of either cited method.
  Role-sign tests, immutable actors and short synchronous microbaselines must
  precede fleet training.
