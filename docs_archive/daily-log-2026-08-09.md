## August 9 strength synthesis

T1 established that cheap/N=30 Teacher choices usually match the expensive
continuation on ordinary states, but the boundary diagnostic is not strong
enough for indiscriminate bulk labels. T3 therefore combines four independent
routes: terminal S3a structured-bury evidence, S4 continuation diagnosis, a
hard-tail Teacher design, and human actions as counterfactually tested
proposals. “Hard tail” is not shorthand for openings: early leads are one
important stratum, alongside bury, follow, late play, role, uncertainty,
proposal disagreement and the two observed point-card mechanisms.

The product objective is explicitly stronger than bot-pool Elo. A challenger
first beats the exact live champion on paired deals, then enters a blinded
candidate-versus-champion test against the same opt-in human cohort, and
finally reports absolute signed level utility against a named experienced-
human cohort. Evaluation games are never recycled into training or selection.
