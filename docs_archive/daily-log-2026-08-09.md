corpus capture, but not yet for a blinded strength claim. Bot policy is chosen
deployment-wide; `round_start` does not bind a room arm, exact policy/image,
assignment probability, consent or pseudonymous evaluation session; mixed
human/bot tables confound partner and opponent effects; and `/data/logs` is the
same source later fetched for training data.

`BACKLOG.md` now makes the prerequisite explicit: a controlled two-human-team
versus two-bot-team layout, hidden immutable room/session assignment, balanced
seat/banker blocks, exact policy and consent/session logging, and a separate
evaluation log root with a second corpus-builder refusal by experiment tag.
The primary comparison is candidate-minus-champion bot-team signed level
utility clustered by player/session. Absolute performance against an
experienced-human cohort is a separate row. No traffic experiment or
production change is authorized in T3.
