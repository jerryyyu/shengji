## Hygiene decisions

- A PR should own one durable output, not a status update.
- A merged branch may still own ignored evidence; remote-branch deletion and
  worktree deletion are separate decisions.
- Experiment stacks consolidate only after their exact runs and review markers
  are terminal and a tested current-main integration exists.
- Documentation has one owner per fact: queue in `BACKLOG.md`, terminal
  results in `AI_POLICIES.md`, rationale in `RL_PLAN.md`, compute in
  `JOBS.md`, chronology here.
- Dead-code cleanup must name the replacement and prove no runtime, CLI,
  registry, import, fixture or artifact-verifier consumer remains.
