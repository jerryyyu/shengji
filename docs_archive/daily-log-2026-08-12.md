## Key synthesized learnings

1. **The pair observation exposed a world-model seam, not a root-ballot seam.**
   Production's root bot already uses public Memory and offered Jerry's `D5
   D5` in VXVS. Ordinary Monte Carlo continuations use `HeuristicBot`, which
   forgets exhausted higher pairs. Changing only that continuation was strongly
   positive in bounded exact endgames.
2. **Recurrence is not human-preference evidence.** The live-log trigger was
   common enough to matter, but humans and bots chose the promoted pair at
   nearly the same observed rate (~20%). The causal evidence is the fresh
   treatment/null exact screen; the census only sizes natural traffic.
3. **A good local continuation can still be diluted by conservative root
   search.** The pair rule activated in 17/24 natural searches but changed only
   one root move. That keeps the hypothesis alive while warning that a uniform
   whole-game screen may need substantial sample—or a tighter natural-dose
   estimand—to see it.
4. **S4 needs prospective accumulation, not retroactive pooling.** Its two old
   estimates can motivate a new sample size but cannot be combined after the
   fact as confirmatory evidence. The two-look successor fixes this before any
   fresh outcome exists.
5. **S6's first HOLD was useful and cheap.** Freezing a Mini-compatible packet
   for an Air job would have made the capacity result nonportable. The v2 packet
   now binds the machine, interpreter and branch-built compiled engine before
   any work begins.
6. **Two-tier rigor is now explicit.** The pair exact test was fast,
   reproducible exploration. Whole-game strength still requires fresh paired
   populations, adequate power, external review and literal live-champion
   comparison.
7. **S6 is a broad ballot expansion, not a rare exception.** On disjoint DEV
   seeds it added a move on 93.4% of natural leads. That meets the coverage
   request but may nearly double lead-search work; only the reviewed preflight
   can determine whether the full experiment is computationally sensible.
8. **S6's broadness is mostly generic whole-holding evacuation.** In bounded
   exact endgames those actions never beat the best existing live-ballot move
   in 128 balanced states. Boss/near bundles at least broke even directionally.
   The actionable split is “always source” versus “selectively spend search,”
   not removing shuai-pai from the ballot altogether.
9. **Pair-cap opportunities were small in count but stable at high N.** The
   repaired live diagnostic found eight such public-proof states, and all eight
   favored the low pair at every 300/1,000/3,000-world checkpoint. That is a
   strong mechanism clue, but shared rooms and multiple states per round make
   it unsuitable as a confidence interval or whole-game strength claim.
10. **Ballot visibility and search spend do not have to be the same thing.**
    S6 can keep all shuai-pai candidates inspectable while reserving a second
    expensive search for the 12.6% of broad triggers in the only stratum with
    positive exact evidence. That converts the broadness problem into a
    testable policy gate instead of deleting requested action coverage.
