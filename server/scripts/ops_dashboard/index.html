Rated rounds = rounds with at least one signed-in seat (casual or ranked; the database does not mark which). Rounds and seat-rounds come from the room logs' round_start records, which name no account. "Human" means human-controlled at round start (not a bot then); a seat can change hands later in the round. A seat-round counts as signed in when a saved-game link covers that seat in that round, counted by the round's own time; the remaining human seat-rounds are the guest estimate. "Today" starts at midnight ET.
