Activity

Rated rounds = rounds with at least one signed-in seat (casual or ranked; the database does not mark which). Rounds and seat-rounds come from the room logs' round_start records, which name no account. A roster seat is one not marked as a bot at round start; that includes a disconnected player's seat while a bot covers it, so roster seats are not evidence of human play. A roster seat-round is signed in when a saved-game link covers that seat in that round (counted by the round's own time), unknown when the database may not have recorded it yet, and otherwise the guest estimate. "Today" starts at midnight ET.
