Ask four people in China what this game is called and you can get four answers. They're mostly the same game. Each name picks out one part of it.

## 升级 (shēngjí): "level up"

The name for the whole game, and the one this site uses. Each team has a level, from 2 up to Ace. You move it up by winning a hand as the defenders, or by scoring 120 or more as the attackers. The level also sets trump: when your team is on 7, the 7s are trump.

```cards
kind: levels
from: 7
to: 9
team: Your team
caption: Hold the attackers to 5 to 35 points as the defenders and your team goes up two levels. That climb is the 升 in 升级.
```

You'll also hear 打升级 ("play level-up") and 双升 ("double level-up") for the two-deck game.

## 拖拉机 (tuōlājī): "tractor"

Named after the combination: two or more pairs in a row of the same suit. The usual story is that a run of pairs laid out in a row looks like a tractor's tracks. It's the name most English speakers learn: Tractor.

```cards
cards: S7 S7 S8 S8 S9 S9
check: combos
trump: H
rank: 2
labels: A tractor of three pairs
caption: ♠7♠7♠8♠8♠9♠9 is led as one play. To beat it in spades you need a higher run of three pairs.
```

[Pairs, tractors and throws](/guides/pairs-tractors-throws) explains how they're followed and beaten.

## 八十分 (bāshí fēn): 80 Points

Named after the target, and called 80 Points in English. The two decks hold 200 points in 5s, 10s and Kings, and the attackers need 80 to win the hand.

```cards
cards: S5 S5 | S10 S10 | SK SK
labels: 5 + 5 | 10 + 10 | 10 + 10
caption: Every 5, 10 and King in one suit, across both decks: 50 points. Four suits make 200.
```

Most players use 八十分 and 升级 for the same game.

## 找朋友 (zhǎo péngyou): "finding friends"

This one is a real variant. It's for more players, usually five or more, with three or more decks. There are no fixed partners. After taking the kitty, the banker names a card, for example "the first ♥A played", and whoever plays it becomes the banker's friend for that hand. Until it appears, nobody knows who is on which side.

That changes how you play. Early tricks are about finding out who's who, and a player can pretend to be the friend, or hide it. The levels and scoring work much as in the four-player game, with the target scaled to the number of decks.

This site plays the four-player game with fixed partners. It doesn't have Finding Friends.

## How tables differ

The core is the same everywhere: two teams, trump set by the level, pairs and tractors, 80 to win. The details change from table to table.

| Rule | On this site | Common elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Players and decks | 4 players, 2 decks with jokers, 25 cards each, 8 in the kitty | 找朋友 with 5 to 7 players and 3 or more decks |
| Partners | Fixed, sitting opposite | Called by a card in 找朋友 |
| Who can declare | Anyone, with a level card or a pair of jokers | The same; some tables rank suits so an equal pair can overcall |
| Nobody declares | Trump comes from the first non-joker card of the kitty | No trump, or a redeal |
| Kitty multiplier | Twice the number of cards in the last lead | Twice the longest part of the lead, or a flat ×2 |
| Failed throw | Only the lowest beatable part is played | The same, plus a penalty of 10 points per card at many tables |
| Levels | Any jump is allowed, up to Ace | Some tables won't let a team jump past 5, 10 or K |
| Winning at Ace | Win a hand at Ace as the defenders | Some tables send a team back to 2 after failing at Ace three times |

When you sit down somewhere new, the questions worth asking are the kitty multiplier, the failed-throw penalty and the rules at Ace. They change the score more than the others.

## Sheng Ji and Guandan (掼蛋): what's the difference?

They share a lot: four players in two teams, partners opposite, two decks with jokers, and levels from 2 to Ace.

They're won differently. Guandan is a race to get rid of your cards: each player has 27, there's no kitty and no point cards. Sheng Ji is played in tricks, and the attackers collect the 5s, 10s and Kings to reach 80. This site plays Sheng Ji.

## Which name to search for

In Chinese, search for 升级 or 拖拉机. In English, try "Tractor" or "Sheng Ji". "Finding Friends" will find the bigger-table variant, and "80 Points" mostly finds the same game you're reading about here.
