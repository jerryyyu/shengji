How to play Sheng Ji (升级 / Tractor / 80 Points)

Also called Tractor (拖拉机), 80 Points (八十分) and 双升. These are the rules as played on this site.

  • 4 players in 2 fixed teams; partners sit opposite
  • 2 decks with all 4 jokers: 108 cards
  • 25 cards each, 8 in the kitty
  • Play goes clockwise: the player on your left plays after you
  • The attackers need 80 of the 200 points
  • Levels go from 2 up to Ace

Players and goal

Four players play in two fixed teams: you and the partner sitting across from you. Each team has a level, starting at 2 (a room's host may pick a later starting level). Winning rounds moves your team up, from 2 through 10, J, Q and K to Ace. A team wins a game by reaching Ace and then winning a round at Ace as the banker's team.

Cards

Two standard decks with all four jokers are shuffled together: 108 cards. Each player is dealt 25 cards and the last 8 cards form the kitty.

Trump

Each round is played at the banker's team's level, called the level rank. These cards are always trump, highest first:

  1. the big jokers;
  2. the little jokers;
  3. the level rank in the trump suit;
  4. the level rank in the other three suits, all equal;
  5. the rest of the trump suit, Ace down to 2.

All trumps count as one suit when following. If the round has no trump suit, only the jokers and the level rank are trump.

Declaring trump

While the cards are dealt, any player may show a card of the level rank to make its suit trump. A stronger show replaces a weaker one: a single level card is beaten by a pair of level cards, which is beaten by a pair of little jokers, which is beaten by a pair of big jokers. A pair of jokers means there is no trump suit. If nobody declares, the trump suit is taken from the first non-joker card of the kitty. In the first round of a game, the player whose declaration stands (the last and strongest one) becomes the banker; if nobody declares, the banker is the player dealt the first card. The banker and the kitty

The banker picks up the 8 kitty cards and then buries any 8 cards face down. The banker leads the first trick. The banker and partner are the defenders; the other team are the attackers. What to bury, and why.

Playing tricks

Play goes clockwise: after you, the player on your left plays. The leader may play a single card, a pair (two identical cards), a tractor (two or more pairs of the same suit next to each other in rank, such as 7-7-8-8), or a throw (several of these together, all in one suit). Everyone else must play the same number of cards. Follow the led suit if you can; if a pair or tractor is led, you must answer with pairs of that suit as far as you hold them. If you are out of the led suit you may play anything, and a trump play of the same shape can win the trick. The highest card or combination of the led suit wins unless it is beaten by trump. The winner leads the next trick.

A throw stands only if no other player could beat any part of it with cards of the suit led. If someone could, the throw fails and the leader plays only the lowest part that could be beaten. A player who is out of the suit led may still trump the whole throw under the normal follow rules; that does not make the throw fail. How pairs, tractors and throws are followed and beaten.

Points

Only three ranks score: each 5 is worth 5 points, and each 10 and King is worth 10, for 200 points in the two decks. The attackers keep the points in the tricks they win. If the attackers win the last trick, they also score the points buried in the kitty, multiplied: twice the number of cards led to that trick (×2 for a single, ×4 for a pair, and so on).

Scoring a round

The attackers need 80 points to win the round. Every 40 points either way moves a level:

  • 0 points: the defenders go up 3 levels;
  • 5 to 35: defenders up 2;
  • 40 to 75: defenders up 1;
  • 80 to 115: the attackers take the deal but do not go up;
  • 120 to 155: attackers up 1; 160 to 195: up 2; and so on.

If the defenders win, the banker's partner becomes the next banker. If the attackers win, the next player after the banker in turn order, an attacker, becomes banker, so the next round is played at the attackers' level. Scoring worked through with real hands.

House rules you may know

The core is the same at every table. The details are not, so here is what this site does where tables often differ:

  • Permanent trump 2s (常主): some tables make every 2 a trump. Here only the jokers, the level rank and the trump suit are trump.
  • Failed throws: many tables add a penalty of 10 points per card. Here the only penalty is playing the lowest part that could be beaten.
  • The kitty multiplier: some tables count twice the longest part of the last lead, or a flat ×2. Here it is twice the number of cards led.
  • Re-burying after an overcall (炒底): here the banker takes the kitty once, after declaring closes.
  • Nobody declares: some tables play without trump or redeal. Here trump comes from the kitty.

More players: Finding Friends

This site plays the four-player game with two decks and fixed partners. Finding Friends (找朋友) is the variant for bigger tables, usually five to seven players with three or more decks. There are no fixed partners: the banker names a card, and whoever plays it joins the banker for that hand. The target is scaled to the number of decks. The names of the game and how tables differ.

FAQ

How many players does Sheng Ji need?

Four, in two fixed partnerships: you and the player sitting across from you.

Is Sheng Ji the same as Tractor and 80 Points?

Yes. Sheng Ji (升级, level up), Tractor (拖拉机) and 80 Points (八十分) are names for the same four-player, two-deck game. Finding Friends (找朋友) is a variant for five or more players with more decks.

Which way does play go?

Clockwise. After you, the player on your left plays.

What is a tractor?

Two or more pairs of the same suit next to each other in rank, such as 7-7-8-8. A tractor is led as one play; the others must follow with that suit, using pairs, if they can.

Can I play against bots?

Yes. The site's own computer players fill any empty seat, so you can play a full game alone or with one to three friends.

Can I play offline?

In the iPhone and iPad app, yes: with no connection it plays a game against the bots on your device.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest by typing a name. Signing in with Google is optional; it keeps your games and gives you a rating for ranked play.

Is it free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy and there are no ads.
